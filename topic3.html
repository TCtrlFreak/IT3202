The Future

The future of the World Wide Web is "Web 3.0". The next generation of the web focuses on decentralization through blockchain technologies, smarter environments through use of artificial intelligence and machine learning, and increased user control. Some of these advancements we are already seeing are non-fungible tokens (NFTs), AI chatbots like ChatGPT, and decentralization allowing users to participate and interact with the web without authorization from governing bodies or from trusted intermediaries.

The future is still yet to come, but Tim Berners-Lee has predictions about what the not-so-distant future will look like. 1: Everyone will have a personal AI assistant. 2: Everyone will take true ownership of their data. 3: A big tech company could get broken up due to monopoly. As artificial intelligence continues to grow at a rapid pace, it is likely that it will become a part of our daily lives just like phones and computers themselves. As technologies continue to integrate into our lives and we become more and more dependednt on them, it would be a massive risk to not have ownership of our data. While tech giants continue to grow, smaller companies are drowned out and monopolies are formed. Even currently there are tech giants that are being investigated for monopolistic practices.

While the future of the web is still being written, it is sure to continue to surprise us and surpase what we thought was possible not long ago.
